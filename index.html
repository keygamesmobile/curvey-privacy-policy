2. General Information

Curvey is a mobile video game for Android devices.

The developer of Curvey does not independently collect personal information such as names, postal addresses, or email addresses for its own marketing purposes.

However, Curvey uses third-party services and software development kits (SDKs) to provide certain game features, advertising, cloud save functionality, and in-app purchases. These providers may process technical information and personal data in connection with their own services.

Curvey uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Unity LevelPlay
  • ironSource
  • Google Play for in-app purchases

3. Local Storage of Game Data

Curvey stores certain game data locally on the player's device.

This may include:

  • Game progress
  • Completed levels
  • Completed difficulty levels
  • Scores
  • Unlocked game content
  • Game settings
  • Other data required for the operation of the game

Local storage is used to provide and maintain game progress and game functionality.

Locally stored data may be deleted when the application is uninstalled.

4. Google Play Games Services and Cloud Save

Curvey uses Google Play Games Services provided by Google.

If the player uses the relevant Google Play Games Services functionality, Curvey may store and synchronize game progress using the Saved Games functionality.

For this purpose, information required to associate a saved game with the relevant Google Play Games player may be processed, together with the saved game data and technical information.

The cloud save functionality is used to back up and synchronize game progress, for example after reinstalling the application or when using another supported device.

Google processes this information in accordance with its own privacy policies.

For more information, please refer to:

Google Privacy Policy

Curvey displays advertisements within the application.

Depending on the advertising service, the player's region, device settings, and applicable consent, information such as the following may be processed:

  • IP address

  • Operating system and application information
  • Advertising or device identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Information about the use of the application
  • Interactions with advertisements
  • Technical diagnostic and performance information
  • Approximate location information, where permitted and required

Such information may be processed for purposes including:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Limiting the frequency of advertisements
  • Providing and maintaining advertising services
  • Personalized advertising where legally permitted and where an appropriate legal basis or consent exists

The respective third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

For more information, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Unity Privacy Policy
